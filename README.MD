# GraysonGains

GraysonGains is a lifting app for tracking your profile, personal records, workout cycles, and prescribed training.

## What it helps you do

- Sign in and keep your training data tied to your account.
- Set up your profile and training details.
- Track your current maxes for the main lifts.
- Build and follow workout cycles.
- See what you are supposed to train on a given day.
- Move through your training progression over time.

## Typical flow

1. Create an account or log in.
2. Finish your profile.
3. Enter your personal records.
4. Set up your cycle and training days.
5. Use the app to review training, progress cycles, and keep everything up to date.

## Main features

- Email/password and Google sign-in
- Profile setup
- Personal record tracking
- Workout cycle setup and progression
- Prescribed workout lookup
- Settings and session tools
